Task 2 - Family Queries

1. ?- female(X).

Result:
X = sara ;
X = nora.

Reason:
Sara and Nora are the female members listed in the family facts.


2. ?- male(_).

Result:
true.

Reason:
At least one male exists in the family facts.


3. ?- parent(Grandparent, Parent), parent(Parent, faisal).

Result:
Grandparent = ali,
Parent = muhammad.

Reason:
Ali is the parent of Muhammad, and Muhammad is the parent of Faisal, so Ali is Faisal's grandparent.


4. ?- parent(ahmad, Daughter), female(Daughter).

Result:
Daughter = sara ;
Daughter = nora.

Reason:
Sara and Nora are children of Ahmad and both are female.


5. ?- parent(Parent, ahmad), parent(Parent, muhammad).

Result:
Parent = ali.

Reason:
Ali is the parent of both Ahmad and Muhammad, so they have a common parent.


6. ?- male(khan), parent(Parent, khan), parent(Parent, nora).

Result:
Parent = ahmad.

Reason:
Khan is male, and both Khan and Nora have Ahmad as their parent, so Khan is Nora's brother.
